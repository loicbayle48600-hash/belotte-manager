# Banque de hooks — pâtisserie parisienne → audience US

## La formule

Un bon hook tient en **une phrase de moins de 12 mots**, prononcée avant la 2e seconde, et
contient au moins **deux** de ces trois éléments :

1. **Un chiffre précis** (jamais rond) : `$14`, `27 layers`, `4:07 a.m.`, `200 grams`, `3 seconds`
2. **Un enjeu** : ce qui se casse, ce qui est jeté, ce qui rate, ce qui coûte
3. **Une adresse directe au spectateur** : `you`, `watch`, `count with me`, `tell me`

Et une règle négative : **le premier frame est un plan du produit fini ou du geste le plus beau.**
Jamais un visage qui dit bonjour, jamais un logo, jamais un plan large de labo.

---

## Prix / valeur

1. "This croissant costs fourteen dollars. Here's the part you're paying for."
2. "Four dollars fifty in New York. Four dollars fifty in Paris. Watch the difference."
3. "He just threw away three hundred dollars of pastry. On purpose."
4. "The cheapest thing in this case took two days to make."
5. "Americans call this overpriced. Then they see the third day."

## Chiffres / process

6. "Twenty-seven layers. Count them with me."
7. "Three days for one croissant. Day one starts now."
8. "Two hundred grams of hazelnut in a single dessert."
9. "He has three seconds to glaze this."
10. "Fifty seconds per croissant. Five hundred a night."
11. "One degree too hot and the whole batch is trash."
12. "Twelve hours of proofing for eleven minutes of baking."
13. "Ninety éclairs before the shop opens at seven."

## Accès interdit / heure

14. "It's 4 a.m. in Paris. This kitchen started two hours ago."
15. "Nobody is allowed back here. Camera got in anyway."
16. "This is what happens in a Paris bakery before you wake up."
17. "By the time you buy it, this has already been touched eleven times."

## Contraste US / France

18. "American bakeries skip this step. That's the entire difference."
19. "This is the number one pastry in Paris. You've never heard of it."
20. "Your croissant is dry because of 4% you can't see."
21. "There's no English word for what he's making."
22. "This would be illegal to sell in most US bakeries. Here's why." *(uniquement si c'est vrai — lait cru, œuf, etc.)*
23. "France's best-selling dessert never crossed the Atlantic."

## Erreur / tension

24. "Watch what he does when it cracks."
25. "This is the moment where it all goes wrong. He does it anyway."
26. "If it looks burnt, he did it right."
27. "He's about to ruin it on purpose."
28. "One wrong move here and three days of work is gone."

## Sensoriel / ASMR

29. "Sound on. That's the only test that matters."
30. "This sound is how you know it's real."
31. "No music on this one. Just listen."
32. "That crack means the lamination worked."

## Question directe

33. "Would you pay fourteen dollars for this?"
34. "Can you see why this one is worth triple?"
35. "Name one US bakery that does this. I'll wait."
36. "Guess how long this took. You're wrong."

## Personnage

37. "He's been doing this for twenty-two years. He still counts every layer."
38. "This man makes five hundred croissants a night and eats none of them."
39. "He fired a batch this morning for 2 millimeters."
40. "Paris has four hundred bakeries. Twelve of them do this."

## Règles d'usage

- **Un hook = une vidéo.** Ne réutilise pas le même hook à moins de 3 semaines d'écart.
- Teste toujours **2 hooks différents sur le même montage** (voir `03-production/plan-de-test.md`) :
  seule la première seconde change, tout le reste est identique. C'est la seule façon de savoir
  ce qui marche sur ton audience.
- Le texte à l'écran du hook ne doit **jamais** répéter mot pour mot la VO — il l'appuie avec
  le chiffre (`$14. ONE CROISSANT.` pendant que la voix dit la phrase complète).
- Un hook qui annonce quelque chose doit être payé dans la vidéo. Un hook non tenu = un
  commentaire négatif et une baisse de distribution sur les vidéos suivantes.
